My experience defending my thesis

Defending my thesis was a very important experience for me because it allowed me to put my research, organizational, and teamwork skills into practice. My role consisted of preparing the theoretical framework and conclusions, as well as reviewing the spelling and writing of the project. I also had the opportunity to present and defend our work together with my classmates.

This experience helped me gain more confidence when speaking in public, learn to take responsibility, and recognize the importance of teamwork in achieving a common goal.

My Experience at Expo Poma

Expo Poma was an experience that taught me a lot about teamwork. During the project, we faced difficulties with communication, organization, and empathy, but despite these challenges, we managed to present our work.

My role was to work on the branding, which was challenging because at times I felt I did not have enough support from my teammates. I became overwhelmed when I struggled to come up with ideas for the logo. However, I kept trying and learned that communication, empathy, and collaboration are essential for achieving good results as a team.

My experience at Expo Tech

Expo Tech was a challenge for me because when I changed to the morning schedule, I also had to join a new group and adapt to a project I did not know much about. At the same time, I had to prepare to retake my Excel certification, so I had to learn how to manage my time and balance both responsibilities.

Although I felt a little lost at first, I found ways to support my new team. I helped my classmate Adriana with the presentation and created the question bank. This experience taught me to adapt to changes, manage my time better, and stay committed even when facing several challenges at the same time.
